## Activity hold, pitch gating and bench timing split (20 November 2026)

Branch `firmware/activity-hold`. Measured block-by-block practice credit through `Analyzer` using the new `tone <ms> <hz> <dbfs> [decay_ms] [gap_ms]` simulator command before changing rules:

- **Before (`rms > gate` per 2.9 ms block, no hold)**: sustained low E2 (`82.41 Hz`) near the `0.0040` gate credited **2,990 / 5,000 ms (59.8 %)** at `-41 dBFS` (cycle RMS `0.00436`), **3,180 / 5,000 ms (63.6 %)** at `-40 dBFS`, and **3,810 / 5,000 ms (76.2 %)** at `-36 dBFS` because harmonic troughs inside a 2.9 ms block dip below the cycle RMS; A2 strums at `-20 dBFS` (`300 ms` decay + `300 ms` chord-change gap over `6,000 ms`) credited **3,060 / 6,000 ms (51.0 %)** and E2 strums (`250 ms` + `300 ms` gap over `5,500 ms`) credited **2,290 / 5,500 ms (41.6 %)**; 107 YIN windows ran during 5 s of stage-4 practice where pitch is not shown.
- **After (`LEVEL_SMOOTH_MS = 40`, `ACTIVITY_HOLD_MS = 500`, `App::wantsPitch()`)**: `App::observe` maintains a 40 ms time-weighted mean-square window (cleared on stale gaps and on `peak >= 0.98` so clipped samples cannot leak into subsequent silence), and `Progress::tick` holds activity for at most 500 ms from the last sample above the gate while zeroing immediately on pause, calibration, tuner entry, clipping, stale observations or `dt > 100`. Sustained E2 `-41..-20 dBFS` now credits **5,000 / 5,000 ms (100.0 %)**; `300 ms` and `400 ms` gapped strums credit **100.0 %** without dropping `active`; `1 s` play + `2 s` silence credits **1,500 ms** (`+500 ms` hold, then `0 ms` for a further `1 s` of silence); stage-4 practice runs **0** YIN analyses (`analyses = 0`, ~1 M float ops saved per 46 ms window). `Record` v1 and `LinkState` (76 B) are unchanged.
- **Telemetry & interrupt handoff** (`VEX_Pocket_Rival.ino`, `analyzer.h`, `link.h`, `tools/bench_report.py`): split RAM canvas + band diff (`draw_us`, `draw_max_us`) from SPI panel transfer (`flush_us`, `flush_max_us`) so the milestone 3a DMA gate measures SPI alone; added `credit_drops` (ticks rejected by `dt > 100` while running with fresh unclipped audio); guarded `Analyzer::takeLevel`, `analyze` and `skip` with `VEX_AUDIO_LOCK` / `VEX_AUDIO_UNLOCK` (`AudioNoInterrupts()` / `AudioInterrupts()` on Teensy, balance-checked in `core_tests.cpp`).

## Critical unknowns

- Header seating is modeled at 9.4 mm; exact socket/pin combinations and access to used pins need bench confirmation.
- Audio board community CAD may differ from purchased D2. The three mount holes and component clearance must be measured.
- Simplified switch/jack bodies do not certify solder-lug or inserted-plug clearance.
- The 32.4 mm screen may be too small for a standing tuner; pet charm can work seated while tuner legibility needs its own test.
- No force rating exists for the printed enclosure. Metal plate/column contact and layer adhesion require a mentor-defined load test.
- Actual pitch stability, ADC gain, USB noise and power-loss behavior have not been characterized.
- Display timing is instrumented but unmeasured: `flush_us` per cadence, tearing at band seams, whether USB serial carries band packets at the 100 ms cadence, and how the motion reads at 32.4 mm. The `make upload` path has not been exercised against a board.
